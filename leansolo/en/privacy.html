Privacy information

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a translation for your convenience: in case of doubt the Italian version prevails.

In short: the shop's invoices and stock stay on the shop's computer and we do not see them. We only process the data needed to let the right people in: the shop's name and VAT number, and the name, email and roles of authorised people.

1. Data controller

The controller of the data described here is Giovanni Luca Tucceri, who develops and provides LeanSolo. For any request: gltucceri@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays in the shop (we do not process it)

LeanSolo is installed on the shop's computer. Electronic invoices, suppliers, products, prices, quantities and stock movements are read and stored only on that computer and are never sent to us. The shop itself is the controller of this data and decides on its use, retention and backups.

3. Data we process and why

DataPurpose
Shop name and VAT numberLinking the installation to the right shop
Email of authorised people and their roles (owner, administration, purchasing, warehouse)Deciding who can sign in and what they can do
Name and email received when signing inRecognising the person signing in
Trial sign-up data: shop name and VAT number, owner's name and emailActivating the 30-day trial and checking that each shop has only one
Computer fingerprint (a code derived from the identifier the operating system assigns to the PC, containing no personal data), activation and expiry dateMaking the licence work on one computer only and counting the trial days
Date and time of sign-ins, technical connection dataSecurity and operation of the service

The legal basis is the performance of the contract with the shop (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the legitimate interest in the security of the service (Art. 6(1)(f)). We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties.

4. Signing in with Google, Microsoft or email

You can sign in with a Google account, a Microsoft account or a code sent by email. With Google and Microsoft, LeanSolo only asks for permission to know your name, email address and account identifier (scopes openid, email, profile): it does not access mail, documents, contacts, calendar or any other account data.

LeanSolo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is used only to recognise the person signing in to LeanSolo.

5. Who helps us (processors)

  • Supabase, for the sign-in service and the list of authorised people, with servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google and Microsoft, only when you choose to sign in with their account.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and the downloadable files.

6. How long

Data of authorised people is kept as long as the shop uses LeanSolo, or until the person is removed from the list; it is then deleted within 30 days, unless the law requires otherwise. Technical sign-in data is kept for the period set by the service providers, and in any case no longer than 12 months.

7. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to object or to receive it in a readable format (Arts. 15–22 GDPR), by writing to gltucceri@gmail.com. To remove your access, just ask the shop owner or us. You can also contact the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools. The LeanSolo program, on the shop's computer, only uses a technical cookie to keep the session open after signing in.
