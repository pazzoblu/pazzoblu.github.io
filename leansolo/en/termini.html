Terms of service

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a translation for your convenience: in case of doubt the Italian version prevails.

1. The service

LeanSolo is a program that reads electronic purchase invoices and updates the shop's stock. It is provided by Giovanni Luca Tucceri (gltucceri@gmail.com). The program is installed on the shop's computer; a central service only manages access for authorised people.

2. Trial version

Versions marked "demo" are for trying the program. They may contain errors, change without notice and must not be used as the only record of accounts or stock. Uninstalling a demo version also deletes the data saved on the computer.

The trial lasts 30 days from the first sign-in with your own shop and works on one computer at a time. If you change computer, you can move the trial from the new computer, up to 3 times; the days keep counting down. After the 30 days you can still view, print and export your data, but you cannot load invoices or change the stock. Each shop (VAT number) can have only one trial.

The trial version is free. The full version will be paid, with a subscription fee: price and conditions will be communicated before purchase, and nothing is charged without an explicit purchase by you.

3. Accounts and access

Access is reserved to people authorised by the shop owner. Everyone is responsible for their own account and PIN and must inform the owner immediately of any unauthorised use. The shop owner decides who can sign in and with which roles.

4. Shop data

Invoices, products and stock stay on the shop's computer; the shop is their controller and is also responsible for backups. LeanSolo does not replace the legal obligations to retain invoices. The processing of account data is described in the privacy information.

5. Proper use

It is not allowed to circumvent the program's protections, analyse its code in order to copy it, resell it or use it for unlawful purposes. Uploaded invoices must belong to the shop the installation is registered to.

6. Liability

The program is provided with the utmost care but without any guarantee that it is error-free. Proposed quantities and prices must be checked by the user before confirming. To the extent permitted by law, the provider is not liable for indirect damage or for data loss not caused by gross negligence.

These terms may be updated: the date at the top shows the latest version. Italian law applies.
